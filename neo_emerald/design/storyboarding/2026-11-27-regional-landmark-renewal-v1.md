## Asset acceptance
Every pack records native indexed palette, metatile/VRAM budget, source files, collision/elevation/behavior, reciprocal warps, preview and engine-ready assets separately. Reuse native tiles where suitable; new art requires approval as appropriate. Avoid gratuitous large maps and avoid mandatory side quests. Maintain all approved routes and plot gates, use existing gear progression and explicit mission contacts, no new trainer IDs without resolving 864/864 capacity. Major new character redesign, legendary history, Bell origins, final outcomes or mandatory plot dependencies require review. Prioritize actual playability of C1–4 before later landmark construction. No artwork or ROM scripts produced in this package.
