## Implementation constraints
- Two early visits are useful and short, not new mandatory detours. First Slateport visit explains the unavailable Gym and when to return, includes optional shops/relief NPCs. First Mauville visit teaches desert safety and enables Route 111 without the fifth badge.
- Preserve BR01–BR16 IDs and completion gates where possible; introduce transit states before BR05, don't reorder badge flags or trainer rosters. Gym badge and report separate prerequisites. Keep current Build 24 C4 endpoint until complete safe onward travel and Relicrest exist.
- Relicrest approved fossil museum/Gym, Lost Stratum rescue, local restoration, later repeatable excavation/minerals and staged fossils; no forced choice affecting campaign. Flint existing six roster preserved; no extra IDs without allocation review. Level curve remains existing until balanced.
- Route access contract: bidirectional native connections/warps, no hidden future badge prerequisites; explicit field equipment authorization, route landmarks and healing; post-visit dialogue explains next objective and local recovery. No false legacy Steven Letter flags. Inspect real map layouts, elevation, collision, weather, warp destinations, legacy scripts before claiming routes.
- Field Log always names destination, person, action and reason; repeatable local contacts/radio reports when safe; no return solely for a hint. When the main story requires returning, change visible town state or give new route/challenge/evidence. Optional fossil excavation never gates the badge or main story.
- Preserve confirmed current Rank 2/Cutter certification after first badge + mission; later rank and tool requirements are not newly fixed here. Audit existing rank constants before new gear gates.
- Major open decisions unchanged: exact Norman evidence, deity blood acquisition, Volcanion outcome, May/Arceus ending, League crisis gate. No early reveals; no late Gym detour during an active irreversible emergency.
- Test each transition both directions, early gate denial, all starters/genders, defeat, save/reload, rewards once, objective text, and every badge. Master untouched. This design is the preferred route for development, not a claim of runtime completion.
