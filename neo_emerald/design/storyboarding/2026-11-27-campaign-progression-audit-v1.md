## Objective display and implementation standard
Use existing Hale board or local mission contact as persistent source of truth. One main objective at a time, with optional quests clearly marked and never silently promoted to main. Each stage records current destination, named contact or landmark, action, requirement, and what happened to previous objective. When a mission is completed show explicit confirmation plus new objective. In remote areas place an equivalent local reminder and clear exit directions; no backtracking solely to retrieve a redundant hint. Do not claim a quest journal/menu exists until built. First implement repeatable NPC text, later reuse approved UI. Suggested text format max native line widths: "NEXT: ROUTE 116 / Find both technicians. / Return to HALE." Match actual native text encoding and widths. Use directional signs only after geometry audit. Dialogue remains skippable/repeatable through contact; no reliance on remembering a cutscene.
