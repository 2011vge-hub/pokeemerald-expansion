# Neo Emerald — Story and experience pillars

**Living design north star.** The user's strongest preference: Pokémon Unbound is beloved because its **story** makes it feel like a **new and fun game**. Pokémon Emerald Imperium is another favorite; it informs combat and QoL. The two are not equal priorities: Neo Emerald must stand on its own as a narrative adventure.

## Creative promise

**Explore a Hoenn that the original heroes saved, but that continued to change without them.** After 25 years, a new generation lives in the consequences of old crises. Nostalgia supplies *contrast*, never a substitute for a new story.

1. **A compelling opening within minutes:** The Mossdeep evacuation introduces flooding, a mysterious suppressed distress signal, ordinary citizens affected by the crisis, and meaningful player involvement. Deliver a gameplay objective rather than prolonged exposition.
2. **A living region worth discovering:** Every redesigned city has its own reason to exist: civilization adapting (Slateport/Mauville), retreating (Dewford), disappearing (Fortree), or being rediscovered (Relicrest). Each new biome teaches a new mechanic and reveals something about the overarching disaster.
3. **Human antagonists, understandable motives:** Neo Magma wants to restore safe land, Neo Aqua wants Hoenn to survive by adapting to the sea, and May's Team Null believes uncontrolled energies must be contained. Their *methods* create collateral damage, but their fear is grounded.
4. **A mystery with emotional stakes:** Professor Birch missing; Norman missing for ~15 years; May made a devastating containment decision, and Champion Brendan knows more than he admits. Preserve uncertainty and contradictions until earned reveals. Do NOT lock Norman's fate or Birch's responsibility prematurely.
5. **Old heroes as consequential adults:** Roxanne teaches in Rustboro, Flannery runs a modern resort Gym, Wally leads Petalburg Gym, Wattson is retired; everyone has changed, not simply aged-up sprites.
6. **Player agency over travel:** The current campaign follows an authored Gym/chapter order, with flexible side exploration, revisits, and rematches. Planned story milestones occur at 0, 2, 4, 6 and 8 badges. Transit links and Ranger field gear replace rigid HM bottlenecks.
7. **Joy and texture between crises:** Side quests with memorable town residents, secret habitats, lighthearted Pokémon moments, exploration rewards, unusual puzzles, small mysteries and friendships. Darkness means little without warmth.
8. **Distinctive battles that serve the fiction:** Our four signature Mega designs are discoveries and lore hooks, not just stat increases. Every boss has readable strengths and ways to counter them. Expert difficulty is opt-in.
9. **A satisfying escalation:** The first act is about survival; the middle about conflicting explanations; the last about realizing the common underlying cause. The eventual solution must cost effort and choices, not erase consequences.
10. **Originality and achievable scope:** Original dialogue, sprites, region layouts and character arcs. Do not copy missions, artwork or set pieces from other hacks. Favor several polished chapters over a giant unfinished map.

## Provisional story progression — NOT final plot canon

| Chapter | Badge milestone | Experience |
|---|---:|---|
| Prologue: The Drowning Signal | 0 | Help evacuate Mossdeep; witness a containment anomaly; first hint that three factions are involved |
| Act I: Cracks Across Hoenn | 0–2 | Travel among altered settlements; discover that each environmental calamity shares strange signatures |
| Act II: The Three Remedies | 2–4 | Investigate competing claims of Neo Magma, Neo Aqua and Team Null; become personally entangled |
| Act III: The Lost Expedition | 4–6 | Trace Birch's work, Norman's disappearance and the history of Team Null, without predetermining their ultimate fates |
| Act IV: The Hidden Fault | 6–8 | Investigate linked earth/sea anomalies; reveal a deeper cause; prepare for choice and consequences |
| Finale and League | 8 | Confront the resulting crisis, then challenge a Champion whose connection to the events is personal |
| Postgame | After champion | Restoration quests, lost-map areas, rematches, legendary mysteries and character epilogues |

## Definition of quality

- A new player can describe the main conflict and feel an emotional reason to continue within 30 minutes.
- A returning Emerald player is repeatedly surprised by the region, without being unable to navigate it.
- Each major chapter introduces a location, character dilemma, a battle/mechanic and at least one optional discovery.
- A player who chooses Story mode is never required to grind competitively optimized teams to advance the plot.
- We do not add an ambitious feature until it fits a stable build and has explicit test criteria.

## Engineering relationship

The near-term blocker is still the **first successfully compiled, emulator-tested ROM** using our fork. The Grimyena prototype is committed but not compiled. Preserve engine stability while story planning continues. Do not claim cutscenes or maps are implemented solely because a narrative outline exists.
