# Pokémon: Neo Emerald — campaign blueprint v2

**Adopted direction:** Eight Gyms in one authored order. Each leader fields a signature Mega. Optional exploration, team building and rematches carry player choice. The main campaign follows chapter events and badge victories. The route, ace levels, mystery resolution and encounter designs below are proposals for playtesting.

## Opening — The last ferry

The player is a rookie Ranger from Mossdeep. On their first real call they have two ferry tickets and one assignment: check names at the upper terminal. The neighbor kid runs back for the family's Poochyena. Dispatch says to hold position. From the platform, the player sees the incoming crew's access stairs collapse. Give control back here. Let the player radio the obstruction, choose Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip at the rescue post, and take the market stairs while they are still passable.

Follow wet pawprints past the shopkeeper to the child. A frightened wild Pokémon blocks the landing; give each starter a viable battle and recovery at the medic. Clip the line to the railing and lower the child to the shop landing. The railing tears loose; salt water floods the powered reel and transmitter. The shopkeeper opens their apartment and you lead everyone across the upstairs balcony to the tram platform. Keep the Space Center ridge visible from all three elevations. On the ferry, the radio still receives. Let the player hear the stranded crews while they walk the deck. The child asks why the siren sounded after the water came in. Retain the time-stamped ferry receipt. Land on the Rustboro-side coast and walk with the survivors to the academy shelter.

## Ranger service and field gear

May led some of Hoenn's best rescues before she and the Rangers who became Team Null broke with command. The rookie and May learn the same lesson at Mossdeep: the person on the ground can see what dispatch misses. In Mauville the player hears why May delayed the siren. Meet both the ranger who cut the locked gate and the shopkeeper who lost their home. When May hears the rookie's account, she asks, “You saw the stairs go down. Dispatch told you to stay. Tell me, Rookie—how long did you wait?” In the finale, the rookie gets the overlooked refuge onto the radio and waits for confirmation from the last boat. This time the people bearing the risk get a voice in the decision.

One recognizable rig gains permanent functions. Field assignments grant equipment while Gym victories measure battle progression. Pokémon retain their moveslots. Dispatch calls lead the player to people who need help; optional calls remain available after the chapter. Log the call and destination so a returning player can pick up the thread.

## Payoff and League

**Proposed answer to the disappearances.** Fifteen years ago Norman signed the order that diverted floodwater into an inhabited district. He stayed behind with displaced families, then kept silent when the League declared the district empty. Birch found the settlement recently and went missing while verifying its history. Norman had years to send word. Give Wally room to be angry with him. Birch returns with testimony; Norman must deliver his own in person.

At Sootopolis, Team Null intends to shut the entire pressure network before another city is sacrificed. May's model saves the islands but assumes the Fortree refuge is empty. The player can prove otherwise. Use Magma's valve maps, Aqua's tide tables and Pacifidlog's boat routes to move the refuge before a staged shutdown. Let the player operate the first gate and escort the first convoy. May challenges the player at the final gate while the remaining ferry crosses. Victory buys time for that crossing. Mossdeep's lower district stays underwater. The people rescued there appear on the quay.

Reopen the route to Ever Grande after the convoy arrives. Proposed League roster: Steven tests defenses and coverage, Wallace tests weather management, Giovanni tests Ground coverage and switching, and Tate & Liza test doubles positioning. Giovanni's League appointment needs a story justification before scripting. Brendan, proposed Champion, uses a mixed team and changes one slot with the player's starter. Target ace level 74. Credits follow the championship and a short visit to the new Mossdeep ferry stop. Postgame: complete Granite Cave's ten floors, recover the original Mossdeep survey archive, and challenge full-strength Gym rematches.

## Player power, difficulty and testing

- Place catchable Poochyena near Rustboro. Level 38 puts Grimyena within reach of the fourth Gym. Grant the player's Key Stone after badge four. Introduce a usable stone and compatible catch together before Mauville. Place Grimyenite in the original Littleroot lab vault during chapter six. Keep Mega opportunities available to players who boxed their starter or skipped Poochyena.
- Reserve Mega Walrein for an optional storm-route rescue and rematch. Permafrost starts hail/snow and boosts Special Attack and Special Defense by one stage at each turn's end while that weather persists. Provide a weather replacement and a stat reset before the fight. Twin Fang and Mega Milotic's ability require design sign-off before locking their boss sets.
- Grow boss rosters from two to six. Use distinct chapter teams and hand-tuned moves. Offer repeatable training, easy move relearning and a heal point before major bosses. First losses should reveal an actionable change. Test on ordinary catches with no trades, rare drops or perfect IVs.
- Prototype each boss with all three starter paths, one team assembled entirely since the previous badge, and one returning team. Track wipe causes and time spent catching up a replacement. If only one counter wins reliably, change the encounter pool or boss set before raising levels.
- Each chapter requires its preceding badge and local story event. On defeat, restore the last safe position and preserve completed rescues. Test save/reload immediately before and after every map change, ferry departure, badge and one-time item. Revisit earlier towns freely once their transport opens.

## Production gates

1. Preserve the boot-tested baseline. Confirm Mightyena's level-38 evolution in battle, party display and save/reload. Finish Grimyena's art, dex and learnset.
2. Build the ferry chapter through Rustboro as a playable slice. Gate acceptance on reaching badge one and returning to the survivors on a reloaded save.
3. Build chapters two through four and player Mega access. Test fresh parties against the first four bosses before freezing level targets.
4. Build chapters five through eight and Sootopolis. Test every faction handoff, transit unlock, Mega interaction and recovery after defeat.
5. Finish League, credits and postgame. Run full fresh-save playthroughs on mGBA and Delta, including a starter-only core and a rotating party. Ship a versioned patch with credits and compatibility instructions.
